Superlatives

The most, the least, and extremely!

🔍 What are superlatives? 🔊

Relative superlatives identify the person, animal, or thing with the highest or lowest degree of a quality in a group. Click the example!

⛰️una montaña alta(a tall mountain)

🪄 Word Order Visualizer 🔊

Relative superlative: article + noun + más / menos + adjective + de. Click the purple word to complete the phrase.

la
montaña
alta
de América

El Aconcagua es la montaña más alta de América.
